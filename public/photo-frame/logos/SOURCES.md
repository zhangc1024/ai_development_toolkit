## Original-color brand assets (2026-10-30)
All 20 built-in brands now have local original-color SVG assets. Apple, Sony, HONOR, Nothing and realme use verified black versions. Nikon's monochrome wordmark remains selectable. No logo is fetched remotely while editing or exporting.
Simple Icons 16.0.0 supplies Apple, Huawei, Xiaomi, Samsung, OPPO, vivo, OnePlus and Motorola geometry under CC0-1.0. Brand hex colors follow its metadata (Huawei uses corporate red #CF0A2C). Sony and Leica reuse the previously documented geometry; Canon reuses its previously documented text-logo geometry with red fill.
iQOO uses the yellow treatment (#FFCA00) of its wordmark. Its Commons source is PD-textlogo. Redmi, realme, Nothing and POCO use the corresponding Commons files. HONOR is the current ring + wordmark from its official site. Google is its official multicolor wordmark.
ViewBoxes are tightened to artwork with a small margin and no geometric distortion. POCO and Fujifilm retain source PNG pixels embedded in SVG wrappers; the presentation window removes surplus padding. Trademark ownership remains with the respective brands.
